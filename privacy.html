Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 August 2026

Polite Carrot ("we", "us") publishes small games for iOS, Android, and the web. This policy covers all of our games. It's short, because there isn't much to say: we don't collect your personal data.

Information we collect

None. Our games do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information — no account, no email address, no device identifiers, no location data.

Gameplay data

Things like your progress, settings, and high scores are saved locally on your own device, using standard local storage. That data never leaves your device, and we never see it. Uninstalling a game removes it.

Third-party services

Our games don't currently include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. If that ever changes for a future release, we'll update this policy and name the service before it ships.

Children's privacy

Because we don't collect personal information from anyone, we don't knowingly collect it from children either. Our games are safe for players of any age from a privacy standpoint.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future game adds an online feature that needs it — we'll update this page and change the date at the top.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email hello@politecarrot.com.
