Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

Polite Carrot ("we", "us") publishes small games for iOS, Android, and the web. This policy covers all of our games and this website. We don't collect anything that identifies you personally — the analytics described below is aggregate usage data, used to understand gameplay and catch errors.

Information we collect

We don't collect anything like your name, email address, or precise location. What we do collect is anonymous usage and diagnostic data through Google Analytics and Firebase — things like which screens or levels get played, session length, device/OS type, and crash or error reports. This helps us understand how the games are actually played and fix what's broken.

Gameplay data

Things like your progress, settings, and high scores are saved locally on your own device, using standard local storage. That data never leaves your device, and we never see it. Uninstalling a game removes it. This is separate from the analytics data described above.

Third-party services

Our games and this website use Google Analytics (GA4) and Firebase to track engagement and understand gameplay and errors. These services may set cookies or use similar identifiers on the website, and use device-level identifiers in the apps. See Google's privacy policy for how they handle that data.

On this website, you can turn analytics on or off at any time below. In the games, open Settings and switch off Analytics to stop tracking on that device — it's off within the app immediately, no need to uninstall.

Website analytics

Google Analytics for this website only. On by default — switch it off any time.

Children's privacy

We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. The analytics data described above is aggregate and isn't used to identify individual players.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future game adds an online feature that needs it — we'll update this page and change the date at the top.
